Privacy Policy

What Get Raw Mixtapes does — and doesn't do — with your information.

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Get Raw Mixtapes ("the App") is a free mixtape app by Gray Code Entertainment, Boston, MA. The App is a wrapper around the website getrawmixtapes.com. This policy explains what the App does with your information. Your account, tapes, plays and downloads are stored on our server, so you can sign in on any device with the same @handle and password.

What the App collects

The App creates an account for you when you sign up or upload a tape. It stores on our server (api.getrawmixtapes.com): your @handle, the display name and city you enter, and your password — stored only as a one-way scrambled hash, never in plain text, and never sent to your phone or to anyone else. When you sign in, the server gives your device a login token that lasts 30 days, so you can sign in with the same @handle and password on any phone or computer. There is no advertising, no analytics SDK, and no in-app purchases, and we do not sell or share your information. We do not ask for your phone number or payment details. You may give us an email address if you want to — it is optional.

Email (optional): you may give us an email address when you create a profile, upload a tape, or in your profile settings — you do not have to. Your email is private: it is never shown on your profile, on tapes, or anywhere other users can see it. If you also tick "Send me promotions from Get Raw", we will use that email only to send you Get Raw promotions (new tapes, featured artists, and other Get Raw news). We never sell your email or share it with anyone else. You can change your email or turn promotions off at any time in your profile, or email info@getrawmixtapes.com and we will remove it for you.

What is stored on our server

When you use the App, the following are stored on our server, not only on your device:

  • Your profile (@handle, display name and city you choose, and your $cashtag if you add one), your saved downloads, and your drafts in My Tapes.
  • Tapes you upload — cover art and audio files — stored on our server from the moment you upload them. Drafts stay private to you until you publish them or schedule a premiere.
  • Play and download counts for tapes and artists, used for Rankings and Year-End 2026 stats. These counts are site-wide — every listener's plays and downloads add to the same totals — and plays and downloads are always counted separately, never combined into one number.

Your phone or computer also keeps a small local copy (the last feed you saw and your login token) so the site can open when you are offline. Deleting the App's data (or uninstalling the App) removes that local copy, but it does not delete your account or tapes from our server.

Deleting your account or a tape: you can delete your account yourself from your profile — tap "Delete my account" and confirm. That permanently deletes your profile and all your tapes (including their cover art and audio files) from our server and signs you out. You can also delete a single tape from My Tapes — tap "Delete" on that tape and confirm. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you would rather have us do it, email info@getrawmixtapes.com and we will remove your account for you.

What the website does

The website you use inside the App:

  • Loads fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Keeps an anonymous, site-wide visit counter (no name, email, address, or device identifier is sent with it).
  • Briefly uses your IP address only to limit abusive requests (too many sign-ins or uploads at once).
  • Links out to Cash App (cash.app) only when you tap a Donate button, and to social media pages. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Uploads

Mixtapes you upload are stored on our server as soon as you upload them, as private drafts only you can see. Publishing makes a tape visible in the App's feed for other users to stream and download, as described on the Upload page. Do not upload music you do not have the right to share.

If you add a $cashtag, it is shown publicly on your profile and tapes, and the Donate button links to cash.app/$cashtag. Donations go straight to you through Cash App — no money passes through Get Raw.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Mixtapes may contain explicit lyrics. If you are under 13, please do not use the App.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the "Last updated" date above will change.
